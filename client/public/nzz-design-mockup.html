NZZ COMPLIANCE AUDIT

English Style Guide & Design Architecture Compliance

1. English Style Guide Rules

  • Headlines: Present tense, sentence case, no terminal period. (e.g., «Smartphones rewire human attention»)
  • Subheads: Sentence case, no finite verbs, no terminal periods. (e.g., «Machines instead of workers»)
  • Quotes: Authentic Swiss guillemets « » with commas/periods inside.
  • Punctuation & Spelling: No Oxford comma; En dashes ( – ) with spaces; US spelling (defense, program).
  • Dates: AP format (Sept. 9, 2026).

2. Visual Hierarchy & Tokens

  • Serif Headings: Newsreader / Iowan Old Style for authoritative editorial presence.
  • Sans Body: Inter / Helvetica Neue at 14–16px with 1.5 line-height for mobile reading.
  • Palette: Paper-50 (#f5f5f5), Paper-950 (#121212), Swiss Red (#d80000).
  • Dark Mode: Natural slate paper (#111111) with high-contrast warm white type.

3. Inspo PHOTO Blueprint

  • Card Structure: Centered NZZ header hairline, crisp 6px radius, neutral dropshadow.
  • Balanced Rhythms: 50% rich photography, 50% data infographics and pull quotes.
  • No Duplicates: Clear separation between lead standfirst and executive brief bullets.
  • Conversion Hook: Final slide provides clear call to action («READ MORE ON NZZ.CH»).
