# Editorial Reader UI: Design Intent & System Specifications

## 1. Editorial Design Philosophy
The primary intent of this reader interface is to project institutional credibility, intellectual weight, and timeless clarity inspired by traditional Swiss broadsheets.
* **Radical Minimalism:** Decorative digital artifacts such as drop shadows, saturated backgrounds, and rounded corners (`border-radius: 0px`) are entirely avoided.
* **Content Hierarchy:** High visual contrast and disciplined negative space direct the reader's attention to news substance rather than interface chrome.

## 2. Color Palette & Semantic Tokens
The palette enforces strict contrast ratios, favoring a clean monochrome base with selective editorial accents.
* **Canvas Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Uncoated pure white creates a stark, authentic newsprint feel that optimizes long-form reading comfort.
* **Ink Black (`#111111`) & Muted Charcoal (`#555555`):** Deep ink ensures high-contrast readability for primary copy, while charcoal softens secondary excerpts and bylines.
* **Editorial Dividers (`#E5E5E5`) & Signal Red (`#D80000`):** Subtle hairline borders isolate layout compartments, while signal red is reserved strictly for live markers and category tags.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy & Micro-Typography
The layout establishes a dynamic tension between an authoritative editorial serif and a functional, modern sans-serif.
* **Headlines (`Playfair Display` / `Georgia`):** Displayed in bold 700 weight with tight leading (`1.1em`) and negative letter spacing (`-1px`) to command immediate authority.
* **Editorial Body (`Lora` / `Merriweather`):** Formatted at regular 400 weight with relaxed line spacing (`1.6em`) optimized for extended, fatigue-free reading sessions.
* **System UI & Metadata (`Inter` / `Helvetica Neue`):** Rendered in clean uppercase sans-serif at small scales (`12px` to `14px`) with expanded tracking (`+0.5px` to `+1px`) for timestamps and section headers.
* **Vertical Alignment:** Text elements maintain strict typographic baseline alignment across column breaks to reflect physical typesetting traditions.

## 4. Grid Architecture & Layout Rhythm
The interface organizes dense reporting through a disciplined, responsive asymmetric grid.
* **Two-Column Asymmetry:** A desktop layout structured into a 70% primary hero zone for leading analysis and a 30% right-hand rail for chronological news wires.
* **Hairline Separation:** Structural compartments rely exclusively on 1px solid dividers rather than cards or surface cards to delineate modules.
* **Dynamic Reflow:** Responsive breakpoints collapse the multi-column canvas into a linear, mobile-first feed below 900px while maintaining strict vertical cadence.
